Built around real work.

MasterCallers is a development practice for organizations that need connected systems, not just another piece of software.

The practice

We begin with the workflow: who needs to act, what information they need, and where the handoff breaks down. From there, we build websites, applications, voice and agent systems, and integrations that help the organization move the work forward.

Training, team support, and curriculum help people understand and run the systems built for them.

Terrence Higgs

Terrence is a founder, pastor, speaker, and software builder. He has built across speaker development, membership operations, professional training, and ministry. SpeakerRealm, MinistryRealm OS, and the other projects in the MasterCallers portfolio reflect a consistent approach: understand the people and the process, then make the technology serve both.

MasterCallers brings that product-building practice to organizations with their own problems to solve.
