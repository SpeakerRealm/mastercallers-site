Call flows and qualification

Understand how MasterVoice handles a conversation and turns it into a usable, reviewable record.

What this session covers

Operators need to know what a voice system is trying to learn, how it asks, and what happens when a caller goes off script. This session walks through inbound and outbound scenarios, qualification criteria, record capture, and the points where the call should transfer or stop.

Your team will learn to

  • Read and explain the opening, discovery, qualification, and closing stages of a call flow.
  • Identify required fields, disqualifying conditions, and what should be left unknown rather than guessed.
  • Handle interruptions, unclear answers, opt-outs, and requests for a person according to the configured workflow.
  • Review call outcomes and recordings or transcripts where enabled and permitted.

Working session

Walk through sample calls with a role-specific flow. Mark what the system captured, what it missed, and when a human should take over.

What you leave with

A reviewed call-flow map and operator notes for common exceptions.

Who it is for

Staff who supervise, receive handoffs from, or improve a MasterVoice deployment.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
