Models and second opinions

Choose the right tool for a task, then use an independent review to find what the first answer missed.

What this session covers

Different models and tools have different strengths, costs, access rules, and failure modes. This session gives teams a practical way to compare them without treating any answer as automatically correct. A second opinion can help challenge a draft, but it still needs source checks and human judgment.

Your team will learn to

  • Match tasks such as research, writing, analysis, coding, and document review to appropriate tools.
  • Compare outputs against the same brief rather than relying on style or confidence.
  • Ask a second model or reviewer to identify omissions, contradictions, and unsupported claims.
  • Keep sensitive material inside the organization's approved tools and handling rules.

Working session

Run one approved task through two available tools. Compare accuracy, usefulness, effort, and what still needs verification before the work can be used.

What you leave with

A simple model-selection and second-review rubric for the team's common tasks.

Who it is for

Staff and leaders who have access to more than one AI tool and need a consistent way to choose and review.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
