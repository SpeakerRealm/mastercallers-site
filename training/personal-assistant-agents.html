Personal-assistant agents

Design a bounded assistant for an individual's recurring work without giving it more authority than it needs.

What this session covers

An assistant becomes useful when its job, inputs, tools, and stop points are explicit. Teams map a real role and identify work the agent can prepare, work it can carry out under supervision, and decisions it must return to a person. The session emphasizes testing and visibility over an impressive demo.

Your team will learn to

  • Choose a narrow task with a clear owner and measurable result.
  • Define the information and tools the assistant may use, including what it must never access or send.
  • Create approval points for messages, record changes, and other consequential actions.
  • Test ordinary cases, incomplete information, and failure paths before wider use.

Working session

Sketch and prototype an assistant for one recurring activity, such as meeting preparation, inbox triage, or drafting follow-up tasks, using sample or approved data.

What you leave with

An agent brief, prototype workflow, and test cases ready for a supervised pilot.

Who it is for

Individual contributors and team leads who want an assistant tied to a specific workflow.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
