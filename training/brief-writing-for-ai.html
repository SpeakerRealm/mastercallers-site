Brief-writing for AI

Turn an operational need into a clear assignment an AI tool can help complete and a person can review.

What this session covers

A useful prompt starts before the prompt box. Teams learn to describe the job, the audience, the material the tool may use, the constraints it must honor, and what an acceptable result looks like. The aim is repeatable work that someone else on the team can understand and improve.

Your team will learn to

  • Translate a vague request into a brief with purpose, inputs, output format, and acceptance criteria.
  • Supply examples and source material while separating facts from assumptions.
  • Test a draft against the brief, spot missing evidence, and revise without starting over.
  • Decide which steps require a person's judgment or approval.

Working session

Bring a recurring task, such as drafting a client response, summarizing intake notes, or preparing a report. Rewrite it as a reusable brief and run a short review cycle on the result.

What you leave with

A reusable brief and a review checklist for one real team workflow.

Who it is for

Teams beginning to use AI in daily operations, and managers who need consistent results across staff.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
