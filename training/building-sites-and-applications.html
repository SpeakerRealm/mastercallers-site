Training / Build

Building sites and applications

Move from a real organizational problem to a working web prototype with a clear path to ownership.

What this session covers

A site or app is more than a screen. Participants work through the user journey, content, data, repository, hosting, and domain decisions that determine whether a prototype can become a dependable tool. The training uses current development tools while keeping the organization's requirements in charge.

Your team will learn to

  • Define the user, the task, and the smallest useful version of the product.
  • Map pages, forms, data, and handoffs before generating code.
  • Understand the roles of a repository, database, domain, and deployment environment.
  • Review accessibility, permissions, testing, and maintenance needs before launch.

Working session

Take one internal or public-facing use case and build a small working flow, such as an intake page or a simple record view. Review what would be needed to make it production-ready.

What you leave with

A working prototype and a practical next-step list for deployment and ownership. Production launch depends on the project's scope and review.

Who it is for

Teams exploring an internal tool, service site, or custom application.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
