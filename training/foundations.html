Training / Lead

Foundations

Give a team a shared, practical understanding of AI before asking it to adopt new workflows.

What this session covers

This session builds a common language around models, prompts, agents, and data. Participants see useful examples and limitations in the context of their own organization, then identify where AI could assist without confusing a plausible answer with a verified one.

Your team will learn to

  • Explain what current models do well and where they are unreliable.
  • Write a basic prompt with context, constraints, and an output format.
  • Distinguish a one-time AI request from an agent that takes steps or uses tools.
  • Recognize data that needs extra care and when an answer requires source or human review.

Working session

Work through a familiar task as a group, compare a weak and strong brief, and identify one suitable pilot workflow.

What you leave with

A shared vocabulary, a starter brief, and a shortlist of sensible uses for the team.

Who it is for

Organizations introducing AI to mixed-experience staff, boards, or leaders.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
