Governance

Set practical boundaries for tools, data, approvals, and accountability across an organization.

What this session covers

As AI use spreads, leaders need decisions that staff can apply in ordinary work. This session helps teams make those decisions visible: which tools are approved, what data may be used, which actions require human approval, and how issues are reported and reviewed. It is a working session, not a legal certification.

Your team will learn to

  • Classify common use cases by the data involved and the consequences of an error.
  • Assign owners for tool access, output review, agent actions, and incident response.
  • Define approval and recordkeeping rules for external messages, customer records, and automated actions.
  • Create a regular review process as tools, policies, and organizational needs change.

Working session

Use several realistic scenarios to draft a decision matrix: allowed, allowed with review, or not approved, with reasons and an escalation contact.

What you leave with

A draft AI-use decision matrix and a list of policy questions for the organization's leadership and advisers.

Who it is for

Leaders, operations owners, and staff responsible for oversight, privacy, and risk.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
