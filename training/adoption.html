Adoption

Move from scattered experimentation to a supported way of working that staff will actually use.

What this session covers

Adoption is a people and process challenge. Leaders choose a narrow pilot, name its owner, train the staff involved, and collect feedback from actual use. The goal is to improve a workflow, not to announce a tool and hope it sticks.

Your team will learn to

  • Choose a pilot with a clear need, manageable risk, and a result that can be observed.
  • Identify champions, affected staff, training needs, and an escalation route.
  • Set a baseline and review usage, quality, time saved, and problems reported.
  • Decide whether to revise, expand, or stop the pilot based on evidence.

Working session

Draft an adoption plan for one workflow, including communication, training, check-ins, and a decision point after initial use.

What you leave with

A pilot adoption plan with owners, feedback channels, and review measures.

Who it is for

Executives, department leads, and project owners responsible for organization-wide change.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
