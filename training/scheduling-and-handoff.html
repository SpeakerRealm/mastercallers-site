Scheduling and handoff

Get qualified conversations into the right calendar, record, and person's queue.

What this session covers

A successful call is incomplete if the next step disappears. Operators trace how information moves from qualification to scheduling and into the organization's existing CRM or work system. They learn to spot missing fields, duplicate records, failed bookings, and handoffs without an owner.

Your team will learn to

  • Map the required information between the voice workflow, calendar, CRM, and team queue.
  • Understand booking rules, availability, confirmations, and rescheduling paths.
  • Check that a qualified record has an owner, a status, and a clear next action.
  • Resolve failed or ambiguous handoffs without silently losing the request.

Working session

Follow sample records through successful, duplicate, and failed handoffs. Identify where the team should be notified and how each case is closed.

What you leave with

A handoff checklist and exception path tailored to the organization's connected systems.

Who it is for

Operators, schedulers, sales or service teams, and anyone who owns the next step after a call.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
