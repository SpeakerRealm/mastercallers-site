Supervision

Run a voice or agent system with clear monitoring, escalation, and change control.

What this session covers

Once a system is in use, someone must know what good performance looks like and when to intervene. This training covers routine reviews, unusual outcomes, escalation, and safe changes to prompts or flows. It also makes ownership visible so the system does not become a black box.

Your team will learn to

  • Review a small set of meaningful measures, such as completed handoffs, unresolved cases, and error patterns.
  • Sample calls or agent actions for quality and adherence to the agreed workflow.
  • Define who can pause a flow, correct a record, approve a change, or contact a customer.
  • Record changes and retest affected paths before resuming wider use.

Working session

Use a sample operator dashboard and exception log to decide which cases need correction, escalation, or a workflow change.

What you leave with

A supervision rhythm with named owners, review points, and escalation steps.

Who it is for

Team leads and designated operators accountable for a deployed voice or agent system.

Delivery and scope are adapted to the organization and can be discussed before the engagement.
