Curriculum

Course and curriculum design as a service and as a licensable asset.

Design service

MasterCallers designs training programs for organizations, churches, nonprofits, and partners. The curriculum is built from current practice: prompting as brief-writing, model selection and second-opinion workflows, personal-assistant agents, agentic systems with defined boundaries, and the practical work of building sites and applications.

Programs can be delivered live or structured for internal use. Format follows the needs of the organization rather than a fixed catalog.

Licensing

Curriculum can be licensed for use by partners, associations, and other organizations that need a ready program without building it from scratch.

Licensing terms are set per engagement. No public price list.

What the curriculum covers

Brief-writing for AI

Turning operational needs into instructions that produce usable output.

Model selection and review

Choosing models for a task and using a second opinion as part of the workflow.

Agents with boundaries

Designing agents that operate inside fences rather than open-ended tools.

Building systems

Practical sequences for sites, applications, repositories, databases, and domains.

Operations

Payments, media, and marketing systems that support the work.

Team adoption

How a staff moves from interest to consistent practice.
